# Placeholder Asset Provenance Notes

The PNG files in `assets/placeholders/` were procedurally generated specifically for this design bundle from geometric primitives. No image file from Mine Bombers or another third-party game was used as a source asset.

They are development placeholders, not a statement about the licensing of the original Mine Bombers game or any third-party property. Before a public release, keep a provenance/license entry for every production asset and replace anything whose rights are unclear.
